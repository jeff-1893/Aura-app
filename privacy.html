Aura — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Aura ("we", "the app") is an anonymous social app organized into private circles. This page explains what information we collect, how it's used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account info: the email address and password you sign up with. This is used only to secure your account and is never shown to other users.
  • Anonymous display name: a name (auto-generated or chosen by you) shown to other members of your circles instead of your real identity.
  • Content you post: text, photos, and voice notes you share in a circle, along with whether you marked it public or private.
  • Circle membership: which circles you've joined or created, and your role (member, co-leader, or leader).
  • Basic usage data: when you last signed in, and whether you installed the app to your home screen. This helps us understand how the app is used.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a post or block another member, we store that action to help circle leaders moderate content.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, or contacts, and we never display your email address or identity to other users. Posting is anonymous to other members by design — your account is only used internally for login security and moderation accountability.

How your content is shared

  • Public posts are visible to every member of the circle you posted in.
  • Private posts are visible only to the specific people you choose when posting.
  • Content is never shared outside the circles you belong to, and never sold to third parties.

Where your data is stored

Account data and posts are stored using Google Firebase. Photos and voice notes are stored using Cloudinary. Both providers process data securely on our behalf and do not use your content for their own purposes.

Moderation

Circle leaders and co-leaders can view reported posts within their own circle in order to review and remove content that violates community standards. They cannot see your real identity — only the anonymous name and the reported content.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own posts at any time.
  • You can block another member to stop seeing their posts.
  • You can leave or be removed from a circle at any time.
  • To request full deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Aura is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Aura grows. Significant changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
